Li Hu

“Advancing Binary Understanding and Software Engineering with AI.”

I am a third-year master’s student in the School of Cyber Science and Technology at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Prof. Nenghai Yu, Prof. Weiming Zhang, and Assoc. Prof. Shaoyin Cheng.

Learning pwn through CTF challenges sparked my interest in understanding low-level program behavior. It also led me to a question: How can we make reverse engineering easier and binary code more readable? I explore AI-assisted binary analysis and reverse engineering, using large language models and program analysis to recover program structure and intent and reduce manual effort.

A related question drives my work on AI agents for software engineering: How can we automate practical engineering tasks reliably? I am interested in agents that reason about code, use tools, and automate tasks such as repository-level compilation and program repair.

I am currently seeking Ph.D. opportunities in AI for software engineering and binary analysis. Please feel free to contact me.
